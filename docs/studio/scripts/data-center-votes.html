Data Center Votes

6 beats · 270 words · about 1 min 48 sec read aloud

Read time is an estimate at 150 words a minute.

Beat 136 words · 14 sec

Five votes in three days

Five local governments voted on data centers between September 28 and 30. Two approved a data center or the land for one. One rejected a zoning change, one banned data centers and one extended a pause.

Beat 250 words · 20 sec

1,260 acres added for a data center

In Cheyenne, Wyoming, the city council voted 8 to 2 to bring about 1,260 acres of Cox Ranch into the city for a data center. SkyBox Data Centers and ViaWest Group plan to build it. The city already has ten data centers operating, five under construction and nine in development.

Beat 347 words · 19 sec

Amazon's 350-acre campus is approved

In Kline Township, Pennsylvania, supervisors voted unanimously to approve a 350-acre Amazon campus near Route 309 and Interstate 81. The approval requires blast surveys of nearby homes before and after construction, and noise testing once it's built. Amazon still needs township zoning permits and state environmental clearances.

Beat 439 words · 16 sec

A $1 billion plan, then a ban

In Lovejoy, Georgia, the developer withdrew a $1 billion data center plan near two schools in August. On September 28 the city banned data centers. The mayor says the developers are now pursuing a residential project on the site.

Beat 546 words · 18 sec

Zoning next to a coal plant rejected

In Mercer County, Kentucky, the fiscal court, the county's governing body, voted unanimously against zoning that would have allowed very large data centers next to the E.W. Brown coal plant. No specific data center had been proposed there. The rules go back to the planning commission.

Beat 652 words · 21 sec

A pause through August 2028

Palm Springs, California, extended its pause on data center applications through August 25, 2028. The city has no rules yet on where they can go, how loud they can be, or how much power and water they can use. Staff say the council would lift the pause once those rules are written.

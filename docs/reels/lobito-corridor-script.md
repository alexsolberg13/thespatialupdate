# Narration script — The Lobito Corridor reel

Short video (~45 sec). Six beats. Read the line, then tap the right side of the
screen to advance to the next beat. Keep it calm and even — the map does the work.

Everything here traces to the story's claim ledger:
`/stories/lobito-corridor/sources/`.

---

**Beat 1 — cold open**
*(on screen: "The Lobito Corridor")*
> Africa's Copperbelt holds some of the richest copper and cobalt on Earth. The only real question is which ocean it leaves by.

**Beat 2 — the Copperbelt**
*(on screen: "A landlocked treasure")*
> This is the Central African Copperbelt. The Congo alone mines about 72% of the world's cobalt — but it's landlocked. Every ton has to cross a continent to reach a port.

**Beat 3 — the old way, east**
*(on screen: "Out to the Indian Ocean")*
> For fifty years, it went east. China built the TAZARA railway in the 1970s to carry the ore to Dar es Salaam, on the Indian Ocean.

**Beat 4 — the new bet, west**
*(on screen: "Back to the Atlantic")*
> Now there's another way out. A US- and EU-backed railway — the Lobito Corridor — pulls it west instead, to the Atlantic port of Lobito in Angola.

**Beat 5 — the missing link**
*(on screen: "A new line for Zambia")*
> And a new line is planned to wire Zambia's Copperbelt straight into it — skipping the long haul east altogether.

**Beat 6 — two oceans, two blocs**
*(on screen: "One belt, pulled both ways")*
> So the same ore can now flow two ways: west to the Atlantic, or east to the Indian Ocean. One mineral belt — and a quiet tug-of-war between two powers over which way it moves.

---

### Timing guide
Roughly 6–8 seconds per beat. Let the map settle after each tap before you start
the next line. Total ≈ 40–50 seconds.

### Tighter cut (~30 sec)
If you need it shorter, drop Beat 5 and trim Beat 2 to: *"The Congo mines about
72% of the world's cobalt — but it's landlocked."*

# Narration script — Revolution Wind reel

Short video (~55–65 sec). Seven beats (cold open + 6). Read the line, then tap
the right side of the screen to advance to the next beat. Keep it calm and
even — the map does the work.

Everything here traces to the story's claim ledger:
`src/stories/revolution-wind/sources.html` (private — open the file locally;
it isn't published to the live site). The map geometry (lease polygon, turbine
points, coastal pin locations) comes from BOEM's official GIS services — see
`dossiers/revolution-wind.md`, "GIS verification pass."

---

**Beat 1 — cold open**
*(on screen: "Revolution Wind")*
> Off the coast of Rhode Island, the federal government drew a line on a nautical chart. What happened next is a story about who gets to erase that line.

**Beat 2 — the law**
*(on screen: "A line drawn on water")*
> This is federal lease OCS-A 0486 — 83,789 acres of ocean, leased by the U.S. government in 2013. It's not a place. It's a legal boundary.

**Beat 3 — the built object**
*(on screen: "65 turbines, standing")*
> Inside that line, 65 wind turbines went up — 704 megawatts, enough power for more than 350,000 homes in Rhode Island and Connecticut.

**Beat 4 — first halt**
*(on screen: "Stop-work, 80% built")*
> Last August, with the project 80 percent finished, the Department of the Interior ordered it to stop — citing national security.

**Beat 5 — one of five**
*(on screen: "One of five")*
> Four months later, a second order hit — this time as part of a coastwide halt on every large offshore wind project under construction, from Virginia to Massachusetts.

**Beat 6 — courts said no**
*(on screen: "Courts said no. Twice.")*
> Federal judges called the security rationale likely pretextual. Every one of the five projects was reinstated.

**Beat 7 — closer**
*(on screen: "Delivering power")*
> By March, Revolution Wind was feeding power into the grid. The turbines outlasted the order that tried to stop them.

---

### Timing guide
Roughly 7–9 seconds per beat, a little longer on Beat 5 (the wide coastal
shot needs a moment to land before you name the other four projects). Let the
map settle after each tap before starting the next line. Total ≈ 55–65 sec.

### Tighter cut (~40 sec)
Drop Beat 3 (fold "704 MW, 350,000 homes" into Beat 2's line) and Beat 6
(fold "courts said no" into Beat 7's opener: *"Courts called it likely
pretextual, and by March, Revolution Wind was feeding power into the grid."*).

### Fact-check before this goes public
- The "80% built" figure (Beat 4) and the exact resumption dates are still
  sourced through news aggregation, not primary documents — see the dossier's
  open C7/C10 flags before this reel is used anywhere public-facing.
- The turbine field shown (Beat 3, 7) is BOEM's filed "Option 1" candidate
  set, not confirmed as-built — see the dossier's C21 flag.
- The four sibling-project pins (Beat 5, 6) are placed at rough BOEM
  lease-area bounding-box midpoints, not precise centroids — fine for a wide
  establishing shot, not for a claim of exact location.

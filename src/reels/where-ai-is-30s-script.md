# Narration script — Where AI actually is, 30-second cut

DRAFT for Harvey to rewrite in his own voice. A 30-second cut of the `where-ai-is` reel
(about 75 spoken words, five beats). Read the line, then tap the right side of the screen
to advance to the next beat. Keep it quick and even; the headline on screen is the hook.

Every sentence is followed by the ID of the claim it rests on, in `dossiers/where-ai-is.md`
(the source record, shared with the longer cut). Delete the `[C#]` tags when you record.
**Rows C6, C10, C11, C13, C16 and C19 are counts or arithmetic on sourced numbers.**

Policy note: this script says where and what, from the sources. It does not argue for or
against AI or data centers.

---

**Beat 1 — cold open**
*(on screen: "Five AI headquarters within about 60 km"; five gold pins around San Francisco Bay, each named; chip "As of Oct 2026")*
> OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and Nvidia all have their headquarters within about 60 kilometers of each other. [C1, C2, C3, C4, C5, C6]

*(on screen: "93 sites, none listed in California"; the ten blue pins appear across the country)*
> Epoch AI, a research group, tracks 93 big AI data centers, and none has a California address listed. [C9, C11, C23] The ten largest in the US are on this map. [C10]

**Beat 3 — the biggest**
*(on screen: "Colossus 2 sits 2,900 km from OpenAI"; sub "Memphis, Tennessee, in a straight line")*
> The biggest of them, Colossus 2, is in Memphis, Tennessee. That's about 2,900 kilometers from OpenAI's headquarters. [C13, C19]

**Beat 4 — Indiana and Ohio**
*(on screen: "Two states hold four of the ten")*
> Indiana and Ohio have four of the ten largest US sites. [C16]

**Beat 5 — what's missing**
*(on screen: "Epoch covers about 43% of AI computing")*

### Timing guide
About 6 seconds per beat. The hook is the on-screen headline of beat 1 (five names, one corner
of California); let the map settle after each tap before starting the next line.

### Fact-check before this goes public
- **Same sources and same caveats as the longer cut** (`dossiers/where-ai-is.md`): power figures and the 43% estimate are Epoch's, as of its Oct 2, 2026 update; refresh before posting.
- **"None has a California address listed"** covers the addresses Epoch gives; 13 of its 93 rows have no address (C11).
- **"The ten largest US sites"** are the ten largest US sites on Epoch's list, ranked by its "Current power (MW)" estimate; two Malaysian sites rank above some of them and are not shown.
- **Pins are geocoded addresses or city centres**, not site outlines (see the Geometry table in the dossier). Meta's and Nvidia's headquarters and New Carlisle are city or town centres.
- **Colossus 2** is in Memphis, Tennessee by Epoch's address, right at the Tennessee / Mississippi line.
- **"Within about 60 kilometers"** is the farthest pair of the five headquarters pins (Anthropic to Nvidia, 60.4 km); Nvidia's is a city centre.
